## Executive Summary
Two isolated wording fixes to four HVE prompt/agent files, ported verbatim from a live-patched
`~/.claude` install: (1) `hve.md` and `hve-plan.md` no longer instruct the agent to invoke a
nonexistent `/think` slash command, replaced with a description of an extended-reasoning pass;
(2) `hve-research.md` and `hve-researcher.md` (both read-only, no-Bash research carriers) gain a
Glob+Read fallback for when Grep is unavailable, with an explicit Bash-workaround prohibition.
All 8 dimension reviews found the core fixes functionally sound, correctly scoped, and free of
security or architectural issues. Findings cluster around polish: an unglossed new term
("interleaved/extended thinking"), inconsistent phrasing between the two near-identical
fallback clauses, and one Major reliability gap — the fallback silently degrades search
coverage with no signal back to the user or consolidated research doc.

## Findings by Dimension

### Major

**RO-001 [MAJOR]** (Reliability and Observability) — `.claude/agents/hve-researcher.md:30`,
`.claude/commands/hve-research.md:47`
The fallback converts a hard block into silent quality degradation: no instruction tells the
agent to flag that Grep was unavailable and that Glob+Read coverage may be reduced.
`hve-researcher.md` has no friction-capture hook at all, so the degraded condition never
surfaces to the parent or the consolidated research document — a user could receive
under-researched findings with no indication anything went wrong.
Follow-on: add an explicit "note fallback usage" clause to both files; consider wiring
`--friction-log`-equivalent capture into the researcher subagent.

### Minor

**FC-001** (Functional Correctness) — fallback scope
Grep-unavailability fallback was added only to the two named research-carrier files; four other
agents share the identical `Read/Write/Glob/Grep`-no-Bash tool profile
(`hve-rpi-validator.md`, `hve-plan-validator.md`, `hve-pr-reviewer.md`,
`hve-prompt-evaluator.md`) and remain exposed to the same failure mode if it's a real harness
condition. **Note:** this mirrors a scope decision the plan already made deliberately (Out of
Scope section: "no evidence any of them share the read-only-with-no-Bash-backstop constraint
that makes the fallback load-bearing for the research phase specifically") — recorded here as a
candidate for a future audit, not a defect in this PR.

**RL-001** (Reusability and Leverage) — `.claude/commands/hve-plan.md:49`, `.claude/commands/hve.md:85`
The new text claims to mirror the precedent phrasing at `hve-review.md:129` but introduces an
unglossaried alias, "(interleaved/extended thinking)", not used anywhere else in the repo
(including `prompts/rpi.md:9`, which just says "extended reasoning").

**RL-002** (Reusability and Leverage) — `hve-researcher.md:30` vs `hve-research.md:47`
The two new Grep-fallback clauses are near-identical but restate the Bash-prohibition sub-rule
with different wording. Since these are the de facto template for any future fix to the other
13 Grep-referencing files, they should share byte-identical wording where nothing needs to vary.

**II-001** (Idiomatic Implementation) — same "(interleaved/extended thinking)" issue as RL-001,
flagged independently: unglossed jargon inconsistent with the corpus's established term
("extended reasoning").

**II-002** (Idiomatic Implementation) — `hve-plan.md:49`, `hve.md:85`
The added "No `/think` command exists..." clause is meta-commentary aimed at future editors,
embedded inside an imperative numbered step — breaks the corpus's action-oriented/present-tense
convention.

**II-003** (Idiomatic Implementation) — same underlying issue as RL-002: the identical
Bash-prohibition phrased two different ways ("do not reach for" vs. "never add ... route
around") is a minor drift from the repo's "same term, same concept" consistency rule.

**II-004** (Idiomatic Implementation) — `hve-researcher.md:6/30`, `hve-research.md:4/47`
The fallback hedges "if no standalone Grep tool is available" even though both files'

**PS-002** (Performance and Scalability) — informational only; the `/think` rewording has no
loop/I/O/scanning behavior and no performance concern.

**RO-003** (Reliability and Observability) — informational; the `/think` wording fix is sound
